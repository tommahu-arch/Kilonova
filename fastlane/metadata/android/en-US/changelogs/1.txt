The first Kilonova release: Supernova with a dock that moves, notifications you can swipe away, and Samsung Finder search.

- Dock drag and drop: icons insert and shift with a live preview and slot targets
- Notification rows in the icon menu: wider, up to 3 lines, swipe to dismiss
- Samsung Finder as an optional search engine (Settings > Search)
- New Kilonova and Kilonova Settings icons
